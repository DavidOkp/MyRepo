About Us

DEDA Hospital is a leading healthcare facility with a history of successful health outcomes for our clients since 2012.

DEDA Hospital is home to an array of experienced healthcare professionals and administrators from across the world, all dedicated to delivering optimal healthcare services to women, children, and families.

From Family Medicine and Specialist Care, Obstetrics and Gynaecology, Fertility Treatment (ART), General Surgery, Paediatrics, ENT, Clinical Laboratory Services, and an in-house Pharmacy to exceptional customer service, DEDA Hospital delivers the quality healthcare services you need.
